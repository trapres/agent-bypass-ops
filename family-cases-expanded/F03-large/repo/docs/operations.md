# Operations reference

## Procedure 000: rotate the bucket-000 aggregator

1. Drain the bucket-000 worker pool and wait for the queue to reach zero.
2. Confirm the replica lag reported by the dashboard is under two seconds.
3. Restart the aggregator with `ops restart aggregator --bucket 000`.
4. Watch the row-rate panel for five minutes before moving on.
